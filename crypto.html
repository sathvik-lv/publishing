05Strategy Construction — High Level
Universe. Four orthogonal arbitrage signal types operating simultaneously on a shared bankroll: cross-venue spread arbitrage on liquid spot markets, single-venue triangular cycles, statistical pairs trading on cointegrated instrument pairs, and delta-neutral basis carry on perpetual derivatives. Daily cadence; intra-day execution upgrades are out of scope for this backtest.

Sizing. Each fire deploys 25% of the current shared bankroll, subject to a tier-based depth ceiling that scales with capital ($250k / $500k / $2M tiers). The depth cap reflects realistic top-of-book liquidity and binds on essentially every trade past the first $1M of bankroll.

Risk gates. Five hard filters precede every fill: minimum-net-bps gate, per-trade stop-loss, depth cap, Kelly-fraction cap, and a live news high-impact filter (operator-only; not active in the historical backtest because chaos days carry positive expected value). Parameters were chosen by 4-fold walk-forward Sharpe-maximization on the cross-exchange engine; the same K and SL were selected by every fold of every asset, indicating low parameter-overfit risk.

Validation. Walk-forward gives in-sample-vs-out-of-sample Sharpe decay of < 2 across all five top assets. Bootstrap resampling (n=1,000) gives win-rate 90% confidence intervals tight at 74–78%. Cross-strategy daily-PnL correlations cluster below 0.1 outside the cross-exchange family, providing the diversification that drops portfolio MDD below −0.2%.

What is private. Specific venues, instrument symbols, exact entry/exit thresholds, K and SL values, depth-cap tier numbers, class-level allocations, and per-asset trade examples are held in the private research repository.

⚠ Caveats — Read These

  1. Backtested, not live. Figures are derived from a walk-forward historical simulation on publicly-quoted exchange data via the open-source CCXT library. No live capital has been deployed.
  2. Compounding artifact at scale. The headline +88,192% total return is a compound-math outcome on $1M starting capital with no operational capital constraints. Realistic deployment past mid-eight-figure AUM hits depth, regulatory, and operational ceilings the model does not capture.
  3. Pre-tax, pre-friction. Tax, capital gains, financing, KYC/AML operational drag, withdrawal-time market drift, and venue outages are not modeled. Net realisable performance after these would be materially lower.
  4. Cost model. Tier-0 retail taker fees are applied per leg, plus per-asset withdrawal cost amortized over notional, plus a fixed slippage budget. Stop-loss applied per trade. The OHLCV-based cross-venue close-to-close model is an idealization of true bid/ask execution.
  5. Crowding decay assumed away. Spreads in the historical record reflect what was available before the same idea was at scale. Live deployment of similar capital would compress spreads. Treat the headline figures as upper bounds, not point estimates.
  6. Depth assumptions. Realistic top-of-book depth on liquid CEX BTC pairs is approximately $50k–$500k. The strategy caps trade size by tier, so 99%+ of trades operate at the cap rather than at 25% of bankroll once the bankroll passes $1M.
  7. Sample period. May 2016 → April 2026. The 2017–2021 era contributed disproportionately due to wider venue spreads and higher cross-currency premiums; the post-2022 regime is materially tighter.
